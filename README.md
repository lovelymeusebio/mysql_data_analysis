# Olist SQL Analysis

## Overview

This project contains a set of SQL analyses using the **Olist** e-commerce dataset. The queries are designed to explore customer behavior, revenue trends, product performance, and category-level revenue contribution.

The analysis focuses on eight business questions covering customer spending, purchasing behavior, monthly revenue, product/category performance, and revenue concentration.

## Business Questions

1. **Who are the top 10 customers by total amount spent?**  
   Identifies the customers with the highest total payment value.

2. **What is the monthly revenue trend across the dataset?**  
   Calculates total revenue by month to understand overall revenue movement over time.

3. **What is the month-over-month change in revenue?**  
   Compares each month's revenue with the previous month and calculates the percentage change.

4. **Which product categories generate the most revenue?**  
   Ranks product categories according to total revenue.

5. **What are the top 3 products within each category by revenue?**  
   Ranks products within each product category and returns the top three products per category.

6. **How can customers be segmented into Low, Medium, and High spend tiers?**  
   Uses customer total sales and `NTILE(3)` to divide customers into three spending tiers.

7. **How many customers are repeat buyers vs. one-time buyers?**  
   Compares customers who placed exactly one order with customers who placed more than one order.

8. **What percentage of total revenue comes from the top category?**  
   Identifies the highest-revenue category and calculates its percentage contribution to total category revenue.

## Key Findings

The SQL queries are structured to provide insights into the following areas:

- **Customer concentration:** Identify high-value customers based on total spending.
- **Revenue trend:** Monitor monthly revenue performance across the dataset.
- **Revenue growth:** Identify months with positive or negative month-over-month changes.
- **Category performance:** Determine which product categories contribute the most revenue.
- **Product performance:** Identify the top three revenue-generating products within each category.
- **Customer segmentation:** Separate customers into Low, Medium, and High spending groups.
- **Customer retention:** Compare one-time buyers with repeat buyers.
- **Revenue concentration:** Measure how much of total revenue is generated by the leading product category.

> **Note:** The provided files contain SQL queries but not the resulting query output. Therefore, specific numerical findings are not stated here. The actual values should be added after executing the queries against the Olist database.

## Recommendations

Based on the analytical areas covered by the queries, the following actions can be considered:

- **Prioritize high-value customers:** Develop targeted retention and loyalty initiatives for customers with higher total spending.
- **Monitor revenue trends:** Track monthly revenue consistently and investigate significant increases or declines.
- **Review month-over-month changes:** Use revenue fluctuations to identify potential seasonality, campaigns, operational issues, or changes in customer demand.
- **Focus on leading categories:** Evaluate the strongest revenue-generating categories for inventory planning, promotions, and assortment decisions.
- **Promote high-performing products:** Consider targeted campaigns and cross-selling opportunities around the top products in each category.
- **Develop customer-tier strategies:** Create different engagement strategies for Low, Medium, and High spend customers.
- **Increase repeat purchases:** Analyze one-time buyers and develop retention campaigns designed to encourage a second purchase.
- **Manage category concentration:** Monitor the share of revenue generated by the top category and consider opportunities to diversify revenue across categories.
